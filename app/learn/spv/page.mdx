# What is an SPV?

A **special purpose vehicle** (SPV) is a separate legal entity set up to hold specific assets, such as a fund company that owns T-bills on behalf of token holders. Tokenized funds are often SPVs or fund companies in places like the British Virgin Islands, the Cayman Islands, or Delaware.

<Callout title="In plain English">
The token is your claim; the SPV is the box that holds the real assets. What you actually own depends on the legal documents for that box.
</Callout>

## What we show

## Why curators care

<Callout title="Why curators care" tone="neutral">
In a default, the token contract doesn't decide who gets paid; the SPV's documents do. If those documents aren't public, you are trusting a summary.
</Callout>
