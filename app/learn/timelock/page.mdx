# What is a timelock?

A **timelock** is a contract that holds a power on someone else's behalf and forces a waiting period between scheduling an action and executing it. OpenZeppelin's `TimelockController` is the common version: proposers schedule calls, and after `getMinDelay()` seconds executors can run them.

<Callout title="In plain English">
Changes are announced in advance. Holders who disagree have time to leave before the change happens.
</Callout>

## How we score it

A timelock inherits the class of whoever can propose through it, plus its delay. A 7-day timelock controlled by a strong Safe is our bar for **green** on upgrades. Shorter delays make upgrades **yellow**.

## Why curators care

<Callout title="Why curators care" tone="neutral">
A scheduled timelock call is advance warning. RWABeat raises a high-severity alert when a call touching a tracked contract is scheduled, so you can act before it executes.
</Callout>
