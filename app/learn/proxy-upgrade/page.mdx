# What is a proxy upgrade?

Many tokens are deployed behind a **proxy**: a small contract that keeps the token's address and balances, and forwards every call to a separate **implementation** contract that holds the logic. Whoever controls the proxy's admin can point it at a new implementation. That is an **upgrade**.

<Callout title="In plain English">
The token's address stays the same, but the rules it follows can be rewritten. A new implementation could add a mint function, change transfer rules, or anything else.
</Callout>

## How we find it

We read the standard EIP-1967 storage slots for the implementation, admin and beacon addresses, and also check for UUPS (`proxiableUUID`) and diamond (EIP-2535) patterns. If the admin is an OpenZeppelin `ProxyAdmin`, we follow it to its owner.

## Why curators care

<Callout title="Why curators care" tone="neutral">
Upgrade power is the most powerful power there is: it can create every other power. Check who holds it and whether a timelock gives you time to react.
</Callout>
