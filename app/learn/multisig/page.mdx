# What is a multisig?

A **multisig** is a smart-contract wallet that only acts when enough of its owners sign. The most common one is [Safe](https://safe.global). A "3/5 Safe" has five owners and needs any three of them to approve each transaction.

<Callout title="In plain English">
It's a shared bank account that needs several people to sign every cheque. One stolen key is not enough.
</Callout>

## What we read

For each Safe we read `getOwners()`, `getThreshold()` and its enabled modules. Modules can act without the owners' signatures, so any enabled module is flagged.

## Strong vs. weak

We call a Safe **strong** if it needs at least 3 signers and at least half of all owners (`m ≥ 3` and `m/n ≥ 0.5`). A 2/3 Safe or a 3/9 Safe is **weak**.

## Why curators care

<Callout title="Why curators care" tone="neutral">
A multisig is only as good as its signers and threshold. Watch for threshold drops (3/5 → 2/5), new owners, and modules being enabled. RWABeat raises an alert for each.
</Callout>
