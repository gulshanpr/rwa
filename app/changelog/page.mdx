# Changelog

Changes to the framework, scoring rules, data and site, newest first. The full history is on [GitHub](https://github.com/gulshanpr/rwa/commits/main).

<ChangelogEntry date="2026-10-30" kind="site" title="A simpler site">

- The asset-class pages are now a filter in the screener. Old `/asset-classes/…` links redirect to `/screener?class=…`.
- The home page shows the assets that need attention, with their worst findings. The screener is the one full list.
- Alerts: filters moved above the feed, with search, severity counts and pages.
- Search ranks tickers first, and addresses appear once you start typing.
- Asset logos appear across the site. Light mode is the default.
- Removed: the JSON download, the `/api/assets` and `/api/alerts` endpoints, and the Learn pages.

</ChangelogEntry>

<ChangelogEntry date="2026-10-26" kind="alerts" title="Lower threshold for large mints and burns">

- Mints and burns now alert above 1% of supply or $5M, down from 5% or $10M.
- The RSS feed and the "suggest a correction" link were removed.

</ChangelogEntry>

<ChangelogEntry date="2026-10-25" kind="framework" version="v1.0" title="Seven risk categories and stages">

- Replaced the six-slice rosette with the RWABeat framework: seven risk categories (Underlying Asset, Legal Claim, Counterparties, Control, Transparency, Pricing, Exit) and Stages 0 to 2. See the [methodology](/methodology).
- New rating state: **unverified**, when a fact isn't public and could make the rating worse. Legal Claim stays not assessed until a legal reviewer joins, so no asset can reach Stage 1 yet.
- Verification became Transparency: graded ladders instead of five pass/fail tests, freshness that depends on the asset type, and onchain data shown as a separate badge.
- Disclosure became Counterparties: providers are rated on independence and regulation, not just on being named. Settlement rails and other critical dependencies count too.
- Control: freeze held by a single key is now red, a 1-of-n multisig counts as a single key, and audits, documented powers and bridges are checked.
- Exit: allowlist-only redemption, daily caps, notice periods and liquidator access are rated. DEX depth is measured for $1M, $5M and $10M sells, with top-10 holder concentration alongside.
- Data: every asset profile gained holdings, maturity and credit facts, audits and bug bounties, transparency reports, and exit terms. Service-provider regulation and auditor tier live on the entity files. Old fields only the rosette used were removed.

</ChangelogEntry>

<ChangelogEntry date="2026-10-14" kind="data" title="Four private-credit assets">

- Added ACRED (Apollo Diversified Credit Securitize Fund, on Securitize), JAAA (Janus Henderson Anemoy AAA CLO Fund, on Centrifuge), mF-ONE (Midas Fasanara ONE, on Midas) and AA_FalconXUSDC (the senior tranche of the FalconX Credit Vault, on Pareto).

</ChangelogEntry>

<ChangelogEntry date="2026-10-13" kind="framework" version="v0.3" title="Business-day pricing and new discovery handlers">

- Pricing: feeds that only update on business days (fund NAVs) don't count weekends towards their age, for both the freshness check and the stale-price alert.
- Pricing: a price reference can name the exact calls that return the value and its update time, for NAV contracts that aren't Chainlink-style feeds (Superstate checkpoints, Ondo `getPriceData`, Centrifuge share prices). The NAV is the same on every chain, so it now prices all of a fund's deployments.
- Control: when several holders share a power, the weakest one (a single key before a multisig) leads the reasons.
- Control: a contract that only holds stop powers (pause, freeze, compliance) over another contract no longer inherits that contract's minting or pricing powers. Previously a 1-of-9 pauser on OUSG showed up as able to mint.
- Discovery: new handlers for Superstate, Ondo, Hashnote and Centrifuge V3. Guards written inline (`_requireAuth()`, `_assertOwner()`) are recognized, and modifiers left in comments are ignored. Centrifuge's shared Hub and BalanceSheet are filtered to the asset's pool.
- Discovery: BNB Chain is supported. Where a chain's logs can't be scanned (BNB Chain, and Centrifuge's spoke chains), holders are only checked among the addresses already found on Ethereum, so those reviews are a lower bound. Discovery prints a warning when this happens.
- Alerts: the watcher reads Centrifuge (wards, pool managers, hook and request manager changes, freezes), Hashnote (roles, capabilities, minters, oracle and teller changes), Superstate (oracle, allowlist, accounting pause, mints and burns) and Ondo (oracles, KYC registry, compliance, router, pauses) events. Tokens with no named mint event alert on large mints and burns from `Transfer`.

</ChangelogEntry>

<ChangelogEntry date="2026-10-13" kind="data" title="USTB, OUSG, USYC and JTRSY">

- Added USTB (Superstate), OUSG (Ondo), USYC (Circle, formerly Hashnote) and JTRSY (Janus Henderson Anemoy, on Centrifuge). Phase 0 reviews are in `research/`; permissions come from the new handlers.
- USTB, Ethereum, block 26,183,258 (3 contracts, 5 holders): one key owns the token and its proxy admin, so it can upgrade, mint, burn from any wallet and swap the price oracle. A second single key writes the NAV checkpoints. $11.7M of USTB is supplied on Aave Horizon.
- OUSG, Ethereum, block 26,183,453 (8 contracts, 10 holders): a 2-of-6 Safe sets the price on the RWA oracle (bounded against SHV), and a 3-of-5 Safe can repoint OndoOracle. A single wallet (0x059b…61a9) can assign or remove holders' KYC IDs, alongside the 2-of-6 Safe. Other OUSG chains are not tracked yet.
- USYC: on Ethereum (block 26,183,723), single keys can upgrade the token, the Teller and the Entitlements contract, and a single key writes the price. On BNB Chain (block 127,400,715), where most USYC now sits, a single key owns the upgradeable token and can mint. That review is a lower bound until a BNB Chain log source is set up.
- JTRSY, on Ethereum, Base, Arbitrum and BNB Chain: one pool manager key writes the NAV and controls minting through the Hub. Balance-sheet managers, including a 2-of-6 Safe, can mint, burn and move shares directly. Protocol-wide changes go through a 48-hour delay held by a 4-of-9 Safe.
- Live data for all four comes from onchain supply × NAV. DefiLlama counts USYC tokens at $1 each, so it isn't used. Morpho doesn't index BNB Chain.

</ChangelogEntry>

<ChangelogEntry date="2026-10-12" kind="framework" version="v0.2" title="Allocate power and vault pricing">

- Control: new critical power `allocate` (lend out or move the assets backing the token). Marking a vault's loans or strategies impaired counts as `set-price`.
- Pricing: vault tokens (ERC-4626) are scored on who can move the contract's own NAV, not on feed freshness.
- Verification: a report with no stated cadence fails the "at least daily" test.

</ChangelogEntry>

<ChangelogEntry date="2026-10-12" kind="data" title="syrupUSDC">

- Added syrupUSDC (Maple), Ethereum deployment. Phase 0 review in `research/syrupusdc.md`; permissions from the new Maple discovery handler at block 26,176,587 (10 contracts, 13 holders).
- The pool delegate is a single wallet that can fund loans, mark them impaired and change who can deposit, redeem and transfer. Its manager contracts can be upgraded with no delay by a 3-of-6 Safe.
- 4 funded Morpho markets ($64M collateral) price it from the vault's own exchange rate. It is not listed on Aave's Ethereum pools.
- Alerts: the watcher now reads Maple events (version upgrades, pauses, admin and pool delegate changes, permission changes, impairments, defaults) and the supply job alerts when a vault's NAV per share falls.

</ChangelogEntry>
