# Changelog

Changes to the framework, scoring rules and data. Every entry links to the pull request that made it once the repository is public.

## Framework v0.1, 6 Oct 2026

- First public draft of the six-slice rosette: Control, Pricing, Verification, Exit, Disclosure, and Legal & insolvency (not assessed).
- Exit: suspension or gating terms that are not public cap the slice at yellow.
- Control: privileged functions no handler recognizes are listed as "unclassified" and don't change the color.

## Data, 11 Oct 2026 (automation)

- BUIDL: permissions now come from the discovery tool at block 26,168,531 (14 contracts, 240 permissions). It runs nightly and opens a pull request when anything changes.
- BUIDL: role-holding DS Protocol services and owned contracts are scored through the keys that control them.
- BUIDL: AUM now comes from DefiLlama (all chains). Supply, oracle age, Morpho and Aave exposure update on a schedule.
- Alerts: the event watcher reports upgrades, owner and role changes, pauses, seizures, large mints and burns, new SEC filings and LEI changes.

## Data, 11 Oct 2026

- BUIDL: replaced all seed data with the Phase 0 review. Profile facts are sourced from Securitize, SEC EDGAR, GLEIF and the BVI FSC.
- BUIDL: permissions are a manual onchain snapshot at block 26,168,303. One EOA can upgrade the token and its services, and holds the MASTER role.
- BUIDL: the price source is the RedStone BUIDL_FUNDAMENTAL feed. Morpho has no funded BUIDL markets. Alerts are empty until the event watcher runs.

## Data, 6 Oct 2026

- Added BUIDL (BlackRock USD Institutional Digital Liquidity Fund) as the first profile, Ethereum deployment only.
- Permissions, oracle readings, DeFi markets and alerts for BUIDL are seed data until the discovery tool and live worker run.
